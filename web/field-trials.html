Rohini to Azadpur with a month of GPS history

For the 5:30 p.m. trip on 24 November, the original Atlas forecast was 6:35 p.m. Google Maps gave 6:28 p.m. When we repeated the search afterwards with the full October model, Atlas suggested the 808STDOWN and 174DOWN, arriving at 6:46 p.m.

GPS placed an 808STDOWN at the pickup stop around 6:29 and at the change stop around 6:30. The next 174DOWN we could trace there came around 7:13. We could not establish when that bus reached Azadpur. The validation report compares all seven November searches and shows which times the buses actually support.

Which direct bus could we trace from AIIMS to Lajpat Nagar?

For the 3 p.m. trip from Laxmi Bai Nagar, Atlas originally suggested a bus change. We found a missing link between the Ring Road stairs and the street. Once we fixed the map, the walk to the eastbound stop was 369 metres, about five minutes. We ran the search again using only bus reports received by 3 p.m.

Atlas put the direct 711UP first. Past travel times put it at AIIMS around 3:06, and the operator’s forecast put the 392UP there around 3:11. GPS later placed the 711UP near AIIMS around 3:05, just as the passenger could have reached the stop. We could not confirm it at Lajpat Nagar.

The first direct bus we could trace at both stops after the walk was the 392UP. It reached AIIMS around 3:12 and Lajpat Nagar around 3:22. With the final 495-metre walk, someone who caught it could have reached the address around 3:29 p.m.

How much longer would a rider wait for the suggested line?

We compared two versions of Atlas in 1,728 searches across six October dates. In 304 searches, GPS showed which line came first and traced the lines both versions suggested at the boarding and exit stops. For someone already at the stop, the median extra wait for the suggested line fell from 3 min 32 sec to about 3 min 5 sec. An untracked bus could have arrived earlier.

Two buses together at Shakarpur School Block

On 22 November, GPS saw two 118EXTUP buses reach this platform from Laxmi Nagar Crossing at 2:18:24 and 2:19:01 p.m., 37 seconds apart. When the second report arrived at 2:19:49, Atlas estimated another 3 min 58 sec until the next bus. The first number describes buses already seen. The second is the forecast a passenger could use.
