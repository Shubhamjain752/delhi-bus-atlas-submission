Compare Atlas and Google Maps for the same Delhi departure. Each forecast uses the planner’s earliest displayed bus journey, including the walk and wait from the requested start time. All clocks are in Delhi time.

On the travel date, GPS can show when a bus passed each stop. The comparison models walking at 1.25 metres per second with no extra transfer allowance, then measures waiting from the modelled arrival at the stop. A passenger’s door-to-door time would require a separate rider record. Google displays its forecasts to the nearest minute.

Which direct bus reached AIIMS first?

On Sunday, 22 November, a person leaving Laxmi Bai Nagar at 3 p.m. could reach the eastbound platform around 3:05:08. Replaying only information received by 3 p.m., Atlas put the 711UP first at 3:05:42 using historical travel times. For the 392UP it had an operator prediction for that particular bus at 3:11:10. Both buses had reported positions west of AIIMS.

The 711 was seen within 27 metres of the AIIMS stop at 3:05:34. Its estimated time of entry overlaps the end of the walk. Its timetable stopping pattern disagreed with the provider, and GPS could not confirm it at the Lajpat Nagar exit. The 392 was seen at AIIMS around 3:12 and at Lajpat Nagar around 3:22. It was the first direct bus we could verify at both stops. Nobody recorded a passenger boarding.
