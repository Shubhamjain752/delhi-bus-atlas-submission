## What changed across Delhi

We repeated 18 searches on the same timetable, walking map and model, including three after midnight. We compared the first three arrival estimates from the old and wider searches before applying the walking preference. Vivek Vihar gained an earlier connection. For the other 17 searches the arrival estimates matched in order wherever three journeys were available, as did the shorter lists. Median local response time rose from 1.46 to 1.81 seconds across these requests. These checks measure what the search finds. Bus punctuality needs GPS observations, and walking times need a rider.

The wider search checks all reachable boarding and exit platforms within the 1.1 km walking limit. To keep response times practical, it tests a limited number of bus changes. There may still be a quicker route. [Routing method](technical-note.md)
