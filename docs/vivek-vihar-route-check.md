The 243DOWN connecting to the 943UP took 10 min 35 sec of walking. The 267UP connecting to the same 943UP needed 13 min 37 sec. Both reached the destination at 12:20:39 p.m. in the forecast. The 243DOWN connecting to the 352DOWN arrived at 12:23:20 p.m. with 10 min 35 sec on foot.

The passenger could reach 24 boarding platforms on foot. The original search checked the nearest 12 for a journey with a change. Yojna Vihar ranked 20th, a 779 m walk away. We changed how journeys are selected so that finding more buses also gives riders a choice of shorter walks.

## What changed across Delhi

We tested the walking preference on 22 selected October and November searches, including three after midnight. Each search used the same available journeys before and after. Previously, five of the 62 choices shown before opening “More options” required at least 20 minutes on foot. With the revised order, one of 62 did. All 20 searches that previously offered at least three choices still showed three upfront.

In 21 of 22 searches a passenger could still select the same earliest predicted arrival. In the evening Rohini to Azadpur search, taking the direct 102ADOWN saved 15 min 2 sec of walking and added 49 sec to the forecast. This comparison measures what passengers see when they search. We did not measure their actual arrival times.

## What the wider search found

We repeated 18 searches on the same timetable, walking map and model, including three after midnight. We compared the first three arrival estimates from the old and wider searches before applying the walking preference. Vivek Vihar gained an earlier connection. For the other 17 searches the arrival estimates matched in order wherever three journeys were available, as did the shorter lists. Median local response time rose from 1.46 to 1.81 seconds across these requests. These checks measure what the search finds. Bus punctuality needs GPS observations, and walking times need a rider.

The wider search checks all reachable boarding and exit platforms within the 1.1 km walking limit. To keep response times practical, it tests a limited number of bus changes. There may still be a quicker route. [Routing method](technical-note.md)
